Quick answers

The things people ask us most.

All questions
What is CuratedbyPriti?

CuratedbyPriti is a handmade and hand-picked gift shop in Wagle Estate, Thane West, Maharashtra. We make soft pipe cleaner bouquets that last for years, framed florals, mirror-work and Lippan wall decor, pearl canvases, illustration portraits and custom gift hampers.

Do you deliver, and to which areas?

Yes. We have delivered in Thane, Bhiwandi, Airoli and many more areas. Message us your area on WhatsApp and we will confirm delivery and timing.

Can gifts be personalised?

Yes. Almost everything can be personalised: photo cards on bouquets, custom bike art panels, portraits drawn from your photo and names added to pieces. Send your photos and ideas on WhatsApp.

What is your returns policy?

Made-to-order and personalised pieces cannot be returned once work has begun. If your gift arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, message us within 24 hours of delivery with photos and we will repair, replace or refund. Unused ready-stock pieces can be exchanged within 3 days. The full policy is on the Returns and terms page.
