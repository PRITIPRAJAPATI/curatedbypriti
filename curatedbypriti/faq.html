Questions and answers

Questions, answered

Everything about ordering, delivery, personalised gifts and returns.

About the shop

What is CuratedbyPriti?

CuratedbyPriti is a handmade and hand-picked gift shop in Wagle Estate, Thane West, Maharashtra. We make soft pipe cleaner bouquets that last for years, framed florals, mirror-work and Lippan wall decor, pearl canvases, illustration portraits and custom gift hampers.

Where is the shop located?

CuratedbyPriti is at Wagle Industrial Estate, Thane West, Thane, Maharashtra 400604 (plus code 5WXX+7J Thane). Walk-ins are welcome, and you can also pick up orders placed on WhatsApp.

What are the opening hours?

The shop is open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm, and closed on Sunday. Hours can change on festival days, so message us on WhatsApp to confirm before you travel.

Ordering and delivery

How do I order a gift?

Open the Order page, tap your choices and your message is written for you. It opens in WhatsApp, where you can add photos and details. You can also message +91 90299 79652 directly or walk in to the shop.

Do you deliver, and to which areas?

Yes. We have delivered in Thane, Bhiwandi, Airoli and many more areas. Message us your area on WhatsApp and we will confirm delivery and timing.

Can I collect my order from the shop?

Yes. You can pick up from our Thane West shop. Choose "Collect from the shop" when you build your order.

Gifts and personalisation

Can gifts be personalised?

Yes. Almost everything can be personalised: photo cards on bouquets, custom bike art panels, portraits drawn from your photo and names added to pieces. Send your photos and ideas on WhatsApp.

Do handmade pipe cleaner bouquets wilt?

No. Our bouquets are made from soft pipe cleaners, so they do not wilt and are made to last for years.

Do you make gift hampers?

Yes, all kinds. Tell us who it is for and what they love, and we put together a hamper for birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, festivals or no reason at all. Ideas for what goes inside include a handmade bouquet, a framed photo, a candle, a mug, a journal, dried flowers, sweet treats and wall decor.

How much do your gifts cost?

Pieces in the collection range from ₹300 to ₹2,199. 11 pieces are on offer right now, with savings up to 36%. The price is shown on every product.

Returns, offers and contact

What is your returns policy?

Made-to-order and personalised pieces cannot be returned once work has begun. If your gift arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, message us within 24 hours of delivery with photos and we will repair, replace or refund. Unused ready-stock pieces can be exchanged within 3 days. The full policy is on the Returns and terms page.

Are there any offers or free gifts?

Yes. Offer prices apply on 11 pieces, loyal customers get a free small gift, and one lucky Instagram follower wins a customised frame free every month.

How can I contact CuratedbyPriti?

Message or call +91 90299 79652 on WhatsApp, send a message on Instagram @curatedbypriti, or email prajapatianu1@gmail.com.

Still have a question?

Message us and we will reply as soon as we can.
